Problem
Alice and Bob recently got into running, and decided to compare how much they ran on different
days. They each ran for N days — on the i th day, Alice ran A i ​ meters and Bob ran B i meters. On
each day –
o Alice is unhappy if Bob ran strictly more than twice her distance, and happy otherwise.
o Similarly, Bob is unhappy if Alice ran strictly more than twice his distance, and happy
otherwise.
For example, on a given day –
o If Alice ran 200 meters and Bob ran 500, Alice would be unhappy but Bob would be
happy.
o If Alice ran 500 meters and Bob ran 240, Alice would be happy and Bob would be
unhappy.
o If Alice ran 300 meters and Bob ran 500, both Alice and Bob would be happy.
On how many of the N days were both Alice and Bob happy?
